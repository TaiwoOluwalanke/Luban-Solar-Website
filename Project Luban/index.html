GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS 05 — FAQ

Before you
make the switch.

Not sure where to begin? These are a few of the questions we hear most.

Ask us directly ↗
How do I know what size solar system I need?+

It depends on the appliances you want to run, how long you use them, and whether you need battery backup. A consultation helps us understand your requirements before recommending a system.

Can solar help reduce my generator fuel costs?+

Solar may reduce generator use when the system is designed for your energy needs. The amount depends on your usage, system size, weather, and how you operate your generator.

Can you support an estate solar project?+

Yes. Luban serves homes and businesses. Estate contractors can contact us to discuss energy requirements for their development and project scope.

What should I share before a consultation?+

Your location, the appliances or equipment you want to power, typical generator use, and any electricity bills or fuel spend estimates are a helpful place to start.

Do you provide installation and support?+

Luban Electrical Services offers solar installation services. Contact our team to confirm the specific assessment, installation, warranty, and aftercare options available for your project.
